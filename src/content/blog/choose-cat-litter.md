# How to Choose the Right Cat Litter

With so many options on the market, choosing the right cat litter can feel overwhelming. Here's everything you need to know.

## Types of Cat Litter

### Clumping Clay Litter
The most popular choice. Forms solid clumps when wet, making scooping easy.

**Pros:** Excellent odor control, easy to clean, cost-effective
**Cons:** Can be dusty, heavier to carry

Our **[Premium Clumping Cat Litter (40lb)](/product/premium-clumping-cat-litter)** is 99.9% dust-free with odor-locking technology — a top choice for odor-sensitive homes.

### Crystal (Silica Gel) Litter
Made from silica gel crystals that absorb moisture and dry out solid waste.

**Pros:** Very lightweight, excellent odor control, less frequent changes needed
**Cons:** More expensive, some cats don't like the texture

### Natural/Biodegradable Litter
Made from materials like pine, wheat, corn, or paper.

**Pros:** Eco-friendly, flushable options available, generally low dust
**Cons:** May not clump as well, can track more

## Choosing by Your Cat's Preferences

- **Senior cats or kittens** — Look for low-dust, unscented options
- **Multiple-cat households** — Clumping litter with strong odor control is best
- **Cats with respiratory issues** — Dust-free natural or crystal litters
- **Pickyp cats** — Start with unscented clumping clay and experiment

## Litter Box Maintenance Tips

1. **Scoop daily** — Remove clumps and solid waste at least once a day
2. **Deep clean weekly** — Empty, wash with mild soap, and refill with fresh litter
3. **Keep it 2-3 inches deep** — Most cats prefer this depth
4. **One box per cat + one extra** — The golden rule for multi-cat homes
