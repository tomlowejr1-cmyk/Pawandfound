## How to Keep Your Cat Happy with Minimal Effort

Cats have a reputation for being independent — and it's well-earned. But even the most self-sufficient feline needs a few key things to be truly happy. The good news? Once you set these up, they practically run themselves.

### 1. Get the Litter Situation Right
Bad litter = unhappy cat = messes outside the box. It's that simple.

- **Use quality litter** that clumps well and controls odour
- **Scoop daily** (takes 2 minutes)
- **Deep clean the box** every 2-3 weeks
- **One box per cat + one extra** is the golden rule

### 2. Provide Scratching Surfaces
Cats need to scratch — it's instinctual. If you don't provide appropriate surfaces, your furniture becomes the scratching post. A simple cardboard scratcher or sisal mat is usually enough.

### 3. Keep Them Entertained
A bored cat is a destructive cat. But you don't need to entertain them 24/7. Just:

- Leave a few toys out and rotate them weekly
- Place a bird feeder near a window (free cat TV!)
- Set up a cardboard box or two (seriously, they love them)

### 4. Fresh Water, Always
Cats are notoriously bad at staying hydrated. A simple fountain (moving water encourages drinking) can make a huge difference to their health.

### 5. Regular Grooming (Even for Short Hair)
Brushing your cat once or twice a week reduces hairballs, keeps their coat healthy, and is a great bonding activity. Plus, less fur on your furniture.

### The Secret to a Happy Cat
It's not about having the most expensive toys or the biggest cat tree. It's about consistency: clean litter, fresh water, regular meals, and a little bit of your time every day. Do those things, and your cat will be perfectly content.
