## 5 Signs Your Dog Needs a New Collar

Your dog's collar is one of the most important accessories they'll ever wear. It holds their ID tags, keeps them safe on walks, and says a lot about their personality. But how do you know when it's time for a replacement?

Here are five signs to watch for:

### 1. Fraying or Worn Edges
If you notice threads coming loose, the edges looking rough, or the material thinning out, it's time. A frayed collar can snap unexpectedly — and that's the last thing you want during a walk.

### 2. It's Hard to Buckle
A collar that's difficult to fasten or unfasten is a safety hazard. Buckles should click smoothly every time. If yours is sticking, rusting, or stiff, replace it before it fails completely.

### 3. Fading or Discoloration
Sun, sweat, and the elements take a toll on collar materials. While a little fading is cosmetic, significant discoloration can indicate the material is breaking down structurally.

### 4. Your Dog Has Outgrown It
Puppies grow fast, and even adult dogs can gain or lose weight. A good rule of thumb: you should be able to fit two fingers comfortably between the collar and your dog's neck. If it's too tight or too loose, get a new one.

### 5. Smells That Won't Go Away
Some collars absorb odours over time, especially if they get wet frequently. If washing doesn't help, it's time for a fresh start.

### What to Look For in a New Collar
When shopping for a replacement, choose one that's:
- **Durable** — genuine leather or heavy-duty nylon lasts longer
- **Comfortable** — padded options reduce chafing
- **Reflective** — for visibility during evening walks
- **The right size** — measure your dog's neck before buying
