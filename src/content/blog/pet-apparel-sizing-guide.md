# The Ultimate Guide to Pet Apparel Sizing

Finding the perfect fit for your pet's wardrobe doesn't have to be a guessing game. Follow this guide to ensure your pup looks stylish and feels comfortable.

## How to Measure Your Pet

You'll need a soft measuring tape. Measure your pet while they're standing on all fours.

### 1. Neck Circumference
Measure around the base of the neck, where a collar would sit. Add one finger's width for comfort.

### 2. Chest Girth
Measure the widest part of the chest, just behind the front legs. This is the most important measurement for most apparel.

### 3. Back Length
Measure from the base of the neck (between the shoulder blades) to the base of the tail. This determines the length of shirts and hoodies.

## Sizing by Breed

| Breed Type | Back Length | Chest Girth | Recommended Size |
|------------|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| Chihuahua / Yorkie | 8-10" | 10-14" | XS |
| French Bulldog / Pug | 10-12" | 14-18" | S |
| Beagle / Corgi | 12-16" | 18-24" | M |
| Border Collie / Husky | 16-20" | 24-30" | L |
| Labrador / Golden Retriever | 20-24" | 30-36" | XL |

## Pro Tips

- **When in doubt, size up.** A slightly loose fit is better than too tight.
- **Check the fabric.** Stretchy materials like cotton blends are more forgiving.
- **Watch for restrictions.** Make sure your pet can move, eat, and potty comfortably.
