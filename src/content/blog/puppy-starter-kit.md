## The Ultimate Puppy Starter Kit: What You Really Need

Bringing home a new puppy is one of the most exciting days — but it's easy to get overwhelmed by the endless checklist of things to buy. We've narrowed it down to the essentials so you can focus on what really matters: bonding with your new best friend.

### 1. Food & Water Bowls
Start with sturdy, non-slip bowls that won't slide across the floor during mealtime. Ceramic or stainless steel are best — they're easy to clean and don't harbour bacteria.

👉 [Shop our bowl sets](/product/bowl-set)

### 2. A Comfortable Bed
Your puppy will sleep a lot — up to 18-20 hours a day! A cozy, washable bed gives them a safe spot to rest. Look for one with raised edges that makes them feel secure.

👉 [Browse pet beds](/product/pet-bed)

### 3. Collar, Leash & ID Tag
Even if your puppy stays indoors most of the time, accidents happen. A well-fitted collar with an ID tag is essential from day one. Choose an adjustable collar that can grow with them.

👉 [Reflective leash](/product/leash-reflective) | [Personalized ID tag](/product/id-tag)

### 4. Grooming Supplies
Get your puppy used to being brushed early — it makes grooming much easier as they grow. A good brush removes loose fur, distributes natural oils, and is a great bonding activity.

👉 [Gentle grooming brush](/product/grooming-brush)

### 5. Toys, Toys, Toys
Puppies explore the world with their mouths. Providing plenty of appropriate toys saves your shoes, furniture, and sanity. Rotate toys to keep things interesting.

👉 [Squeaky ball](/product/ball-squeaky) | [Plush fox toy](/product/toy-fox)

### 6. Gentle Shampoo
Puppies get messy. A mild, puppy-safe shampoo is a must for bath time. Look for something pH-balanced and free of harsh chemicals.

👉 [Gentle pet shampoo](/product/shampoo-gentle)

### The Bottom Line
You don't need everything in the pet store on day one. Start with these essentials, and add as you go. Your puppy will love you for the attention and care — not the stuff.
